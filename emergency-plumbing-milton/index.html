When a plumbing problem needs urgent attention

Emergency Plumbing Milton calls are for situations where waiting could allow a problem to continue or make the home harder to use. A significant leak, water where it should not be, or a sudden plumbing failure are reasons to call and describe what is happening. If you are uncertain, explain the symptoms rather than trying to diagnose the cause yourself.

If it is safe and you know how, you can turn off the relevant water supply while you arrange help. Keep away from water near electrical equipment, and do not take apart plumbing to search for a fault. Give the plumber a clear account of what you see and when it began.

What to do while arranging help

A few practical steps can make it easier to explain an urgent plumbing issue and avoid making it worse:

  • Identify the affected room or plumbing fixture.
  • Say whether the issue is ongoing, intermittent, or has stopped.
  • If safe, move belongings away from water and contain a small amount with towels or a container.
  • Use a shutoff only if you can identify it and operate it safely.
  • Keep children and pets away from the affected area.

Avoid using a fixture that appears to be causing an overflow or leak. Do not apply sealants or force a stuck valve as a quick fix. If you have already tried something, tell the plumber what you did so they have the full picture.
